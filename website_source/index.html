III. The idea a small manifesto

I take something ordinary and return it mythic.

Pets become cosmic beings. Moods become oceans. Your future self offers perspective. Memories become something you can hold.

Each tool is a small act of attention, the moment technology stops being cold and becomes something close to tender. Some of what I make will land quietly. A few people will hold it close and never say a word. Some of it will travel further than I expected. All of it counts.

Always asking the same question: does this make someone feel something real?

IV. About Irina the keeper of the cabinet

Making things that feel like love

I'm a product designer and artist, and an agent of delight. I build small apps that sit at the intersection of AI and emotion. My tools are personal, playful, and designed to make you feel something real.

Delight seemed too soft a word to build a life around. It wasn't.

irina.love is the home for everything I'm making, one experience at a time.

— irina
