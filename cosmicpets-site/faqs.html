FAQs

Frequently asked questions

If your question is not here, just email me. I answer everything myself.

Do you use AI to generate images?

No. These are not AI generated images. Every cosmic portrait is created with royalty-free images that are digitally altered and layered by hand, to build beautiful worlds for your beloved pet.

What kind of pets do you do?

Pets come in all shapes and sizes and I am not fussy. As long as there is a good picture of them, anything is possible. See the photo guide for what makes a good picture.

Do you accept commissions for children and adults?

At the moment this is not something I am considering. The concept of Cosmic Pets works well with all your furry and feathered friends.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, though it will add cost and shipping time. Get in touch to discuss your options. I am happy to accommodate.

Is this an automated service?

Not at all. Every order is very hands on and made from scratch: art, illustration and design, mixed media, framing, shipping, all of it.

Do you do any other sizes than A3?

I certainly do. Custom sizes from A5 up to A1 are available. Get in touch and we will work out what suits your wall.

Do you offer discounts?

There are no general discounts at the moment, but you get 10% off when you order two or more portraits.

What is your art style?

Good question. I would say it is mostly digital collage and printed mixed media. You can see how it comes together on the about page.

How long does a portrait take?

You will get a preview within 5 working days of sending me your photo. After you approve it, printing, framing and shipping take a few days more depending on the package you chose.

Is there someone I can talk to about my pet portrait?

Absolutely. You can talk to me at hello@cosmicpets.co.uk. I will be more than happy to help.

Still have a question?

Ask me anything. I would rather answer ten questions than have you order something you are unsure about.
