Order & contact

Tell me about your pet

Whether you want to order a portrait or just have a question about how it all works, costs, framing or shipping, this is the place. I read and answer every message myself.

Let’s talk

I would love to chat about my art and my creative process, so if you have any questions at all, please do not hesitate to get in touch.

I am still figuring out how to keep costs down while delivering joyful work, so if you have feedback on how I can make this better for you, I would love to know.

And if you would just like to tell me your favourite story about your pet, I would love to hear all about it.

Prefer email?
hello@cosmicpets.co.uk

Or say hello on Instagram
@cosmic_pet_portraits

Before you send: have a quick look at the photo guide. Getting the photo right is most of the work.

Send your photo by replying to my email, so it arrives at full quality. Please do not attach it here.

What happens next

I will reply to your message, usually within a day or two. Send me your photo in that reply, and I will get to work. You will see a preview within 5 working days, and nothing gets printed until you love it.
